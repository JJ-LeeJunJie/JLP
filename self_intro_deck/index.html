The 2026 EC Rule Reset

Same "EC" label, two very different commitments

On 8 May 2026, Singapore reset the rules for new Executive Condos. Flip the switch to see exactly what changed.

Examples: Solano Grand, Wynwood Grand, Sembawang Road EC and Woodlands Drive 17 EC (Sim Lian) — all tendered before 8 May 2026, so they keep the old framework.

Rules are determined by the Government Land Sales tender closing date, not the launch date. Separately, EC sites with tenders closing on or after 24 August 2026 carry a higher S$18,000 income ceiling (S$16,000 for earlier sites). I confirm which rules apply before we shortlist any project.
